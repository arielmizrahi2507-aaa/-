Other graphics
--------------
The other icons (menu, close, check, arrow, accessibility, speech bubble) were drawn for this site. The "柔" mark
is a character from the Shippori Mincho font inside a red circle; the favicon and the Open Graph image are built
from it. None of these is the logo of WhatsApp, Waze, Google or any other company, and none is an official logo
of the club.

No JavaScript libraries, no external stylesheets, no analytics and no tracking scripts are used.
